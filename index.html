Controls

Keyboard + mouse (Windows)

  • W / S or ↑ / ↓ forward / back
  • A / D or ← / → turn
  • Q / E strafe left / right
  • Space ascend · C descend
  • Shift boost
  • V camera · mouse drag to look · wheel to zoom
  • Esc pause menu

Gamepad (Xbox-style)

  • Left stick: forward / strafe
  • Right stick: turn / look up-down
  • RT ascend · LT descend
  • A (hold) boost · Y camera · Start pause

Touch (Android)

  • Left stick: forward/back and turn
  • Right stick: up/down and strafe
  • BOOST (hold) · ◎ camera · ☰ menu
  • Drag empty screen to look around
