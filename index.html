Controls

Keyboard + mouse (Windows)

  • W / S or ↑ / ↓ forward / back
  • A / D or ← / → turn
  • Q / E strafe left / right
  • Space ascend · C descend
  • Shift boost
  • F or left click fire (Gundam: beam rifle · dragon: fireball)
  • V camera · mouse drag to look · wheel to zoom
  • Esc pause menu
  • On foot (HUD shows WALK after a slow landing on the ground or a roof): W / S walk · Shift run · A / D turn · Q / E sidestep · Space jump and take off · fire as usual
  • Difficulty (start screen or Settings): Easy arcade · Medium · Hard - full gravity and momentum; turn by banking (A/D rolls, the bank stays until you roll back); boost drains the BOOST gauge

Gamepad (Xbox-style)

  • Left stick: forward / strafe
  • Right stick: turn / look up-down
  • RT ascend · LT descend
  • A (hold) boost · Y camera · Start pause
  • RB or X fire
  • On foot: the same sticks walk, turn and sidestep · A (hold) run · RT jump and take off

Touch (Android)

  • Left stick: forward/back and turn
  • Right stick: up/down and strafe
  • BOOST (hold) · FIRE · ◎ camera · ☰ menu
  • Drag empty screen to look around
  • On foot: left stick walks and turns · BOOST runs · right stick sidesteps, push up to jump and take off
